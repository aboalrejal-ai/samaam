# Samaam — 4-Minute Demo Script

**Read at a calm pace.** **557 spoken words — 3:50 at a normal pace, 4:17 if you read slowly.**

Time yourself once. If you land over four minutes, drop the **Knowledge base**
section (0:35–1:05, 78 words) and go straight from the opening to the console.
It is the only self-contained block, and cutting it brings you to about 3:20.
Everything else builds on what came before.

Do not rush to fit. The pauses are part of it. Every hard word has a pronunciation note. Nothing here needs
medical knowledge — you are describing what the screen does.

**Before recording**
- Both servers running. Check the sidebar reads **Connected · 43 · 6 · 1338**
- Browser at **1920×1080**, zoom **125%**, light theme, English
- Console page open, nothing loaded yet
- **Do this once as a rehearsal.** The block is instant; the explanation takes
  about forty seconds and arrives while you keep talking. Do not wait for it.

---

## 0:00 – 0:35 · The problem

> **[Show: the console, empty]**

"This is Samaam. In Arabic, *samaam* means a valve.
 (say: sa-MAAM)

It sits between a radiology technologist and a CT scanner.

A technologist on a night shift enters settings for a scan. The patient has
cancer, and chemotherapy has weakened their kidneys. The settings look normal.
For this patient, they are not — and studies show errors peak at the end of a
night shift.

Nothing between that screen and the machine reads the patient's chart. Samaam
does."

---

## 0:35 – 1:05 · The knowledge base  *(the cuttable block)*

> **[Click: Knowledge base. Type in Arabic: متى يوقف الميتفورمين؟]**

"Every decision comes from this. Forty-three verified records from Saudi
primary sources — the Ministry of Health, the Food and Drug Authority, the Data
Protection Law.

I am asking in Arabic when a diabetes medicine must be stopped before a scan. It
returns the Ministry protocol first, page twenty-eight, with a link to the real
document.

This is retrieval, not a chatbot. Ask it what it does not cover and it returns
nothing — which is an answer."

---

## 1:05 – 2:20 · The block

> **[Click: Console → SC-02 → point at eGFR]**

"A sixty-two year old cancer patient. This case was written by a radiologist.

This number is her kidney function.
 (point at **19.1**)

Nineteen. Severely reduced. Samaam calculated it itself, using the formula
printed in the Ministry protocol, page twenty-one.

Tired, the technologist picks the standard adult protocol. The technologist
sends it to the machine."

> **[Click: Send to the device]**

"Watch the rail.

The request travels the seven nodes of the ITU standard — and then it stops.

**Four hundred and three.** The scanner never received it.

But read what it says. **Held for review. Not a prohibition — a radiologist
decides.**

That wording matters. No kidney number forbids this scan outright. A system that
says *prohibited* at a lab value is replacing the doctor, and that is the thing
doctors reject most.

Four findings, and each says what it needs. Red means a named radiologist must
approve. Amber means the operator confirms and continues.

The red square badge is a legal matter — the radiation dose is above the
national level. The amber ones depart from a Ministry protocol: national, but
guidance, not a penalty. Samaam never mixes those two up.

Every finding carries its source, and a link that opens the real document."

---

## 2:20 – 2:55 · The override

> **[Click: Override and release → type a name → confirm]**

"So a radiologist reviews it — and releases it by name."

> **[Show: HTTP 200, then point at the rail]**

"The scan proceeds. The valve reopens — and keeps a scar. That mark stays.

This is the gap we found in Saudi regulation: nothing says who is responsible
when a tired technologist accepts an automated recommendation. Samaam does not
fix the law. It makes sure someone's name is on the decision."

---

## 2:55 – 3:25 · The hard block

> **[Click: Data request → Submit request]**

"One more. A service account is trying to export four thousand patient records
out of the Kingdom, for a marketing campaign.

Three violations of the Data Protection Law. Session terminated.

And notice — I sent the same consultant's name. **Refused.**

A consultant can accept clinical risk for their own patient. No doctor can
permit what the law forbids."

---

## 3:25 – 4:00 · Close

> **[Click: Gap matrix, scroll slowly. Then Audit trail.]**

"Six regulatory gaps, each one a text we searched for and did not find.

And the audit trail — every decision, with a name against it.

One last thing. The block took twenty-nine milliseconds. The written explanation
took forty seconds, because that part uses a language model.

They are separate on purpose. **The model never decides.** Remove it completely
and Samaam still blocks correctly, every time.

That is the whole idea. The AI is not trusted. It is constrained.

Samaam. Thank you."

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
| :--- | :--- |
| Explanation card still loading at the end | Ignore it. Say the line about it being separate — it becomes the point |
| Sidebar dot turns red | Backend stopped. Restart it and re-record from 1:05 |
| Running long | Drop the Knowledge base section entirely — see the note at the top |
